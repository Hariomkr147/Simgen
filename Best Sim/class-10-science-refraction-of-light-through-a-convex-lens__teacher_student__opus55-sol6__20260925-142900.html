Nature of image

Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f
Also: m = h′/h = v/u;   P = 1/f (f in metres), in dioptres (D).

What to observe

  • Object beyond 2F₁: image lies between F₂ and 2F₂; real, inverted and diminished.
  • Object at 2F₁: image forms at 2F₂; real, inverted and the same size.
  • Object between F₁ and 2F₁: image lies beyond 2F₂; real, inverted and magnified.
  • Object at F₁: refracted rays are parallel; the image forms at infinity.
  • Object between F₁ and O: image forms on the object's side; virtual, erect and magnified.
  • A shorter focal length gives a larger power in dioptres.

Check your understanding

1. An object is at 2F₁ of a convex lens. Where does the image form?

  1. At F₂
  2. At 2F₂
  3. Beyond 2F₂
  4. At infinity
Reveal answer

At 2F₂. With u = −2f, 1/v = 1/f − 1/(2f) = 1/(2f), so v = 2f and m = −1.

2. A convex lens is used as a magnifying glass. Where is the object placed?

  1. Beyond 2F₁
  2. At F₁
  3. Between F₁ and O
  4. At 2F₁
Reveal answer

Between F₁ and O. Then v is negative, so the image is virtual, erect and magnified.

3. What is the power of a convex lens of focal length 10 cm?

  1. +0.1 D
  2. +10 D
  3. −10 D
  4. +1 D
Reveal answer

+10 D. P = 1/f = 1/(0.10 m) = +10 D; it is positive for a convex lens.
