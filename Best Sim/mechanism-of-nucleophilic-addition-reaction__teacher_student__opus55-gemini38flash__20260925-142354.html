Rate Law: Rate = k [R₂C=O] [CN⁻]
Arrhenius: k = A · exp(-Eₐ / RT)  (A = 10⁹ M⁻¹s⁻¹)
Base Catalysis: [CN⁻] = C_HCN · Kₐ / (Kₐ + [H⁺])  (pKₐ = 9.2)
What to Observe:
  • Hybridisation Shift: Planar sp² carbonyl carbon (120°) puckers into a tetrahedral sp³ alkoxide (109.5°) upon nucleophilic attack.
  • Base Catalysis: Raising pH above 9 dramatically ionises HCN, boosting [CN⁻] and acceleration.
  • Reactivity Order: HCHO (Ea = 40 kJ) > CH₃CHO (50 kJ) > CH₃COCH₃ (60 kJ) due to steric hindrance and +I electronic relief.
  • Temperature Effect: Increasing T exponentially raises rate constant k.
